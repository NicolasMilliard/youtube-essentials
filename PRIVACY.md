# Privacy Policy — YouTube Essentials

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

YouTube Essentials is designed with privacy as a core principle.

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or analyze personal data.

## Data collection

YouTube Essentials does not collect personal or sensitive user data.

The extension does not use:

- Analytics
- Tracking
- Advertising
- User accounts
- Remote servers
- External APIs
- Telemetry

## Local preferences

YouTube Essentials stores extension preferences locally using the browser's extension storage
API (`chrome.storage.local` in Chrome and `browser.storage.local` in Firefox).

These preferences currently include:

- Whether Shorts are hidden
- Whether Shorts remain available on creator channel pages
- Whether Playables are hidden
- Whether featured videos are hidden
- Whether the watch-page sidebar is hidden (Quiet watch)
- Whether opening the YouTube homepage redirects to subscriptions
- Your selected appearance theme

These settings remain on your device and are not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Website access

YouTube Essentials runs only on YouTube pages.

Access to YouTube is used solely to modify the interface according to your preferences, such as hiding Shorts, Playables, featured videos, and related navigation or recommendation elements.

## Website content processing

To identify the interface elements that should be hidden, YouTube Essentials processes a limited amount of YouTube page structure locally in your browser.

This may include inspecting DOM elements, labels, attributes, and links associated with Shorts, Playables, featured videos, or related interface controls.

This information:

- Is processed only on your device
- Is not stored as browsing data
- Is not transmitted to the developer
- Is not transmitted to third parties
- Is not used for analytics, profiling, or advertising

YouTube Essentials does not collect or transmit your:

- Viewing history
- Search history
- Google or YouTube account information
- Video activity
- Comments
- Subscriptions
- Personal information

## Network requests

YouTube Essentials does not make external network requests.

No browsing data, website content, or extension data is sent to the developer or third parties.

## Data retention

The developer does not retain user data because YouTube Essentials does not transmit or remotely store user data.

Preferences stored locally by your browser remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or the extension is uninstalled.

## Third parties

YouTube Essentials does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

## Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of YouTube Essentials changes.

Any change involving data collection, processing, or transmission will be reflected in this policy and in the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons privacy disclosures.

## Contact

For questions, issues, or privacy concerns, please open an issue in the YouTube Essentials GitHub repository.
